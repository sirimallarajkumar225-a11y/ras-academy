Server RAS Diagnostics: Evidence-Based Troubleshooting

Reliability, Availability and Serviceability (RAS) practices help teams understand faults, evaluate system impact and support recovery. Effective diagnostics begin with evidence and a disciplined investigation process.

A layered view

Consider hardware, firmware, operating system, device drivers, workload and platform management as related sources of information. No single log or layer necessarily tells the whole story.

Investigation workflow

  1. Define the reported symptom and scope
  2. Collect timestamped, read-only evidence
  3. Correlate sources and identify trends
  4. Separate observed facts from hypotheses
  5. Propose safe next steps and validation
  6. Record findings in a concise evidence report

Useful Linux evidence sources

Depending on the system, kernel logs, journal records, PCI topology, CPU and memory information, and platform management records may provide relevant context. Tool availability and output vary.
